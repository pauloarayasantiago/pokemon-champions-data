### Known quirks

- **Env-var loading order:** `lib/supabase.ts:loadRootEnv()` loads `.env` into `process.env` at its module init. Anything else that reads `process.env` must either (a) be imported AFTER supabase.ts or (b) read lazily at call time. `lib/rerank.ts` uses (b) — `getJinaKey()` instead of a top-level const.
- **Jina latency:** ~2.9s per query observed. Single `fetch()` call, Vercel-compatible.
- **Score scale:** Post-Jina scores are in ~0.3–1.7 range (after boostMul=20 on top of Jina's 0–1). Pre-Jina fallback is ~0.02–0.12 (RRF + boosts at 1x). Don't mix comparisons across these modes in debugging.
