# Dragonite Held Item Options — Champions

For Dragonite in Champions, here are your best held item options — note Dragonite has two excellent ability options (Multiscale for bulk, Inner Focus for Fake Out immunity), and your item choice should reinforce that plan.

## Mega route (65% usage — the default)
- **Dragoninite** (Mega Stone): Locks Dragonite into Mega Dragonite, boosting BST to 700 with Multiscale kept. This is the single most common held item for Dragonite and usually the best choice if your team has a Mega slot for it.

## Non-Mega sets — top held items from tournament data
- **Lum Berry** (~15% usage): Auto-cures any status. Strong with Dragon Dance/setup sets because one Spore or Will-O-Wisp doesn't shut you down.
- **White Herb** (~4%): Restores one stat drop. Great into Intimidate spam (Incineroar), or after firing Draco Meteor.
- **Dragon Fang** (~3%): 1.2× Dragon-type moves. Solid power boost for Draco Meteor / Dragon Pulse sets.
- **Silk Scarf** (~2%): 1.2× Normal moves — specifically pairs with **Extreme Speed** for priority KOs.
- **Sitrus Berry** (~1.5%): 25% HP restore at half HP. Generic bulk item, works on Multiscale sets that expect to eat one hit.
- **Focus Sash** (~1%): Guarantees a survived hit from full HP. Fragile-lead Dragonite sets.
- **Leftovers** (~1.6%): Passive 1/16 recovery.

## Unavailable in Champions — do NOT try to run these, they are not in the format
- Life Orb (missing), Choice Band and Choice Specs (missing), Heavy-Duty Boots (missing), Loaded Dice (missing — no Scale Shot boost item). If you've read older VGC guides recommending Life Orb Dragonite, that's Scarlet/Violet — it doesn't apply here.

## Recommendation priority
If your team has the Mega slot, run **Dragoninite**. If the Mega slot is taken by something else (Mega Clefable, Mega Gengar, etc.), run **Lum Berry** on a Dragon Dance set or **White Herb** if you're facing a lot of Intimidate.

Sources: `pikalytics_usage.csv` (Dragonite top_items column), `items.csv`.
