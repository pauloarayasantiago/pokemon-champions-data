Hello everyone. Priority has been around since Pokemon's creation, and it's more important in Scarlet and Violet than ever before. Terra allows sweepers to suddenly gain new coverage or enough power to muscle past Pokémon that would normally stop them. Worse, they sometimes use a defensive Terra to set up even more. The days where fast Pokémon and choice scarfers could deal with sweepers are over thanks to Terra. In singles, priority is the only way to have a consistent team that can handle these sweepers. With the exception of full stall, a team without priority is a bad team. But a team with priority is not necessarily good, and all priority is certainly not equal. If you throw sucker punch on your lead, does that satisfy the priority requirement? Well, it might put your mind at ease, but it won't work so well in a battle. The metag game is already well adapted to King Gambit sucker punch and Samurro's sucker punch is much much weaker. You're not going to stop hardly any sweepers with that. What priority is best to avoid getting swept? That is the point of this video. I'm going to be ranking and discussing every meta relevant priority user. Before we get into the ranking though, let me explain the judgment criteria. First of all, this ranking will be based on performance in the scarlet and violet overused tier, the main format for competitive singles. Smokcon has a usagebased tiering system where the most used Pokémon are in the overused tier or U for short. Below that is underused or uyu, rarely used or ru, and so on. Third, this ranking will be based on how effectively these priority Pokémon KO sweepers or other offensive threats. That means we assume the priority user has a safe entrance to the field. And we're asking ourselves, how likely is this priority user to successfully KO this sweeper breaker or offensive threat? Since most safe switchins on sweepers and other offensive threats involve a Pokemon dying first, I'll be calling this revenge killing pretty frequently throughout the video. Because we're assuming the Pokemon has safe entry, the Pokemon's defensive strengths won't be considered much unless it's relevant to the sweeper that you're entering on. Generally though, we are ranking these Pokemon on how likely they are to enter on a sweep or an offensive threat and instantly kill with their priority move. There are three major factors that make priority moves better or worse. The first is power. The stronger the better, obviously. The next is reliability. Can it be used at any time or does it have conditions like sucker punch and first impression? Are there any immunities to the move the Pokémon could terra into? Basically, how often will this move successfully come off and hit your opponent? The last is the metag game surrounding that priority move. How many common sweepers or offensive threats resist the priority or are weak to it? Do a lot of sweepers have better physical defense or special defense? If a priority move is powerful, reliable, and well suited to the metag game, like Lowkick's first impression, you have a fantastic sweep stopper. If your priority move is weak, unreliable, and heavily prepared for, like Dragapult, Sucker Punch, you have a nearly useless sweep stopper.
