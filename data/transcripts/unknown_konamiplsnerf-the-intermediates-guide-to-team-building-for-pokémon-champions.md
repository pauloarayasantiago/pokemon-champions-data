Cores are similar to partners. However, they involve a group of Pokémon that can synergize super well with each other while also adding to your team's win condition. The great thing about a lot of cores is that they usually cover each other both offensively and defensively. The most well-known core that you probably heard of is the fire water grass balance core which has been around in VGC for years. Water types are able to cover for grass type weaknesses such as fire or ice. Grass types can cover for fire type weaknesses like water or ground and fire types can cover for water type weaknesses like grass. This doesn't mean that you can just throw any Pokémon of these types onto a team together and call it a core necessarily. They should still be able to synergize. I think an excellent example with the Pokémon that currently exist in Pokémon Champions when it comes to finding a core include Palafin, Incineroar, and Sinistcha. This is a very beginner core that is similar to the balance core used at the beginning of Scarlet and Violet. Palafin wants to switch out as soon as possible to activate zero to hero so it can be a huge damage dealer for your team. There are numerous play styles when it comes to team building which can help create your win condition or open up a second one if need be. A lot of newer trainers are still getting used to the process of distributing EVs to their Pokémon which isn't always as cut and dry as you might think. Most new players might be tempted to throw all of their points into their speed stat wanting to be as fast as possible or invest all of their points into HP to help live attacks. But most of the time a lot of those EV points are wasted when it's not being used properly. To effectively use EVs or effort value points on a Pokémon, it's best to use them with a specific purpose in mind. Whether you want to outspeed a certain speed tier or Pokémon, you could put only the EV points you need and reinvest the rest of those EVs somewhere else. Let's take Talonflame for example. It has a faster speed stat than Whimsicott who are known for wanting to run max speed to get their prankster increased moves before other Whimsicott and force speed ties. So instead of investing maximum speed into Talonflame, we can instead make our Talonflame 1 point faster than Whimsicott and transfer those extra points to a different stat like HP or attack to help confirm kills or take hits better, especially into said Whimsicott with priority boosted brave birds or dual wing beats.
