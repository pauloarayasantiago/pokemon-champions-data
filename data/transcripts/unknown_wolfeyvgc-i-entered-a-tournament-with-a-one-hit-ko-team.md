A onehit KO move is a move that KO's anything in just one hit. In theory, it should be the pinnacle of all moves. A move that ignores typing, stats, and even the Pokémon itself to deliver an inescapable doom seems like the kind of thing that keeps competitive players up at night. But it doesn't. Because onehit KO moves across the board are terrible. There's a simple reason why. The odds of a onehit KO move actually hitting an opponent in competitive play are a piddley 30%. Unlike other moves with low accuracy, there is no way to increase the odds of a one-hit KO move actually hitting. Raising a Pokémon's accuracy, lowering a fo's evasion, even setting up the field condition gravity, all have no effect at all. These moves are stuck at 30% accuracy no matter what you try. Unless, of course, you had a way to bypass accuracy altogether. See, there's an ability called No Guard, which at one point was the exclusive ability of the Machamp line. When a Pokemon with no guard uses a move or is targeted by a move, that move cannot miss, no matter what. And this includes the powerful onehit KO moves.
