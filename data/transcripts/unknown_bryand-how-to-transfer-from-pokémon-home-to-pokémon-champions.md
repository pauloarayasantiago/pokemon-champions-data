In order to send Pokémon from Pokémon HOME to Pokémon Champions, you have to open Pokémon HOME and click Pokémon Champions in the bottom right corner. You have to have already made your Pokémon Champions account before doing this. Once you open it up using the same Nintendo Switch profile, we're then confirming that you [music] want to connect your Pokémon HOME account with your Pokémon Champions account. If you have more to transfer in, you can add those in later as well. While a Pokémon has been moved to Pokémon Champions from Pokémon HOME, it will stay in HOME, but you won't be able to move it from your boxes to other games. So, you'll be able to import Pokémon, but they will be imported sometimes with moves that they do not have in Pokémon Champions.
