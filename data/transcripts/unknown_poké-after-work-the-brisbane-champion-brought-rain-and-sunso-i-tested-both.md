# The Brisbane Champion Brought Rain AND Sun—So I Tested Both

**Channel:** Poké After Work | **Date:** unknown | **Views:** 112 | **Duration:** 8:14

---

6 HP on Garchomp. Excadrill is still standing with Sandstorm still activated and I need one more Earth Power. Kieran String's Brisbane winning six gives me Pelipper for rain, Charizard for sun, and Mega Garchomp Z. I wanted to see how those choices fit together. Do I commit to one plan at the start or keep a weather setter for later? In the first game, I bring Charizard, Venusaur, Pelipper, and Arcaludon. Raichu and Rillaboom are across from my Charizard and Venusaur. Raichu mega evolves. I protect both slots because either lead can threaten fake out and both fake outs come out. Against what they actually clicked, attacking would have lost both actions. Protect buys me a clean turn to set sun. Rillaboom gives way to Milotic. I mega evolve Charizard. Sun comes up. Venusaur lands sleep powder on Raichu. Now the sun pair has room to attack. Raichu leaves. I try sleep powder on Milotic to deny hypnosis. It misses, but heat wave removes Rillaboom and hypnosis misses back. Could I have used leaf storm instead? It pressures Milotic directly, but I can't call a knockout from this health. And the special attack drop weakens the next hit. The miss alone doesn't make sleep powder a bad choice. Then Gholdengo comes in. Heat wave burns Milotic, but hypnosis finally connects and Venusaur falls asleep. Charizard spends a turn protecting. A little later I pull it back for Pelipper. Rain replaces sun and Charizard stays available to reset it. Leaving Charizard in might preserve sun now, but risks the setter I could need later. Venusaur wakes up and leaf storm removes Milotic. Pelipper puts up tailwind. That is a lot more than a weather icon. The switch gave me another way to play the next board. Raichu comes back nearly gone. I switch Pelipper out for Charizard and sun comes back, too. Venusaur finishes Raichu, then Gholdengo knocks Venusaur out. This is a real trade, not a free weather reset. Arcaludon takes the empty slot. Charizard's weather ball hits Gholdengo and the screen gives me a critical hit. Gholdengo goes down. I'll take the win. That battle rewarded keeping both weather setters. For the next one, I leave Charizard at home and try the Garchomp mega instead. I lead Grimmsnarl and Garchomp into Sneasler and Tyranitar. Salamence comes in for Tyranitar. I protect Garchomp to cover Sneasler's hit while Grimmsnarl sets reflect for later physical pressure. Close combat goes into protect. So, Garchomp takes no damage on this support turn. Now, both sides mega evolve. Salamence gets its mega. Garchomp fights, Z activates, and my Garchomp answers with Draco Meteor. Salamence drops. That's a huge swing, but the game carries on from here. I bring Pelipper in get rain on the field. Grimmsnarl gets put to sleep. But, a later hurricane from Pelipper takes Sneasler out. The support Pokémon are doing real work here. Pelipper uses wide guard into Indeedee and Tyranitar. Expanding force and rock slide both run into it. I could have used hurricane to pressure one target, but both spread moves would threaten my side. On this turn, wide guard covers the moves they actually chose while Grimmsnarl puts up light screen. Garchomp comes back much later. A second Draco Meteor removes Indeedee. Arcaludon still has an attack to make. Then the opponent forfeits and that's the win. Mega Garchomp got important knockouts but reflect Pelipper and wide guard are part of why this game got that far. Last game I lead Grimmsnarl and Arcaludon into Tyranitar and Indeedee with Pelipper and Garchomp in back. Reflect goes up before Indeedee tricks away light clay. Tyranitar knocks off Arcaludon's leftovers but flash cannon chips it back. Indeedee leaves for Excadrill and Tyranitar protects. I add light screen. Flash cannon hits protect. Reflect is already covering the physical pair. Light screen was my hedge against Indeedee staying in. I switch Grimmsnarl for Pelipper as Arcaludon picks electro shot. Rain appears then Mega Tyranitar immediately brings sand back. After high horsepower and rock slide electro shot only charges. I wanted the immediate rain attack but the weather timing took it away. Pelipper comes back out for Grimmsnarl. Arcaludon survives high horsepower and low kick then it's charged electro shot hits Tyranitar. On the next turn Arcaludon protects. Excadrill takes Grimmsnarl down to 30 HP and Tyranitar's knock off is blocked. Grimmsnarl's parting shot weakens Excadrill and brings Pelipper back. Rain finally sticks for this turn. Rock slide leaves Arcaludon on 1 HP and Tyranitar's low-kick finishes it. Garchomp enters. Tyranitar switches to Indeedee as I mega evolve Garchomp. Excadrill protects. Earth Power halves Indeedee, and Pelipper's Weather Ball goes into protect. Good damage, but Excadrill remains untouched. Excadrill switches to Tyranitar. Sand returns. I pull Pelipper for Grimmsnarl. Garchomp's Earth Power crits Indeedee and finishes it. At the end of the turn, that opening reflect finally wears off. Garchomp protects, and Grimmsnarl puts reflect up again. Close Combat hits protect. Sand then knocks out Grimmsnarl, bringing Pelipper and rain back. That second reflect stays. Excadrill protects. Pelipper uses Wide Guard. And Garchomp knocks out Sneasler with Earth Power. Wide Guard doesn't block anything here. The payoff is Sneasler gone. Tyranitar returns. Sand overwrites rain, and Pelipper uses Wide Guard again. Excadrill lands Iron Head, but Garchomp fires Earth Power and knocks Tyranitar out. Sand remains, and Excadrill is the last obstacle. Iron Head makes Garchomp flinch. Hurricane chips Excadrill. Next turn, both Excadrill and Garchomp protect while Pelipper sets Tailwind. Sand leaves Garchomp on 6 HP, and the second reflect wears off. One attack left. Excadrill tries protect again, but it fails. From 6 HP, Garchomp gets Earth Power off and wins. I couldn't count on that failure when I selected the move. So, rain or sun? For the Charizard and Venusaur lead, I'd open with sun and keep Pelipper for later. That let me switch to rain, use Tailwind, and still bring Charizard back to reset sun. Against Tyranitar, my first rain switch lost the weather order, and Electro Shot had to charge. Reflect, parting shot, the later rain reset, and Garchomp kept that game alive. My answer is to bring the weather setter for the turn it matters, not just the opening. Which four would you bring?
