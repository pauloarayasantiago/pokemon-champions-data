Each Reggie is the sole avatar of a single typing, either rock, steel, or ice. This isn't inherently a problem, but unfortunately, the types of two of the original three reggies are terrible. Rock and ice are types that can be strong, but they aren't here. And that's because the Reggies have stats that entirely gear them towards defense.
