First and most importantly, we're going to talk about who's setting trick room. Obviously, Farigiraf is going to be one of them. I think there's been too much positive PR on Farigiraf recently. I also have Sinistcha as an alternate trick room setter, and I gave it a Focus Sash item just to make sure I cannot get one-hit KO'd. I basically have two Pokémon to help support me set up trick room if I think my trick room setter is going to immediately die. The first and most obvious one is going to be Mawile. For Mawile before it Mega Evolves, I'm using the ability Intimidate, which is going to lower the attack stat of both foes upon switching. This Pokémon is already super beefy, and it gets access to one super helpful supporting move being wide guard. Hisuian Avalugg has a really high defense stat while at paper than special defense stat. So, instead of investing in making it bulkier on the physical side, I decided to go all out on making sure that it could survive hits from special attacks. And then for my other moves, I'm running rock slide, body press, and mountain gale. Mountain gale is a move that has 120 base power, but only 85% accuracy and has a chance to make my opponent flinch. And I decided to use this move over something more consistent like ice spinner because I actually decided to give my Hisuian Avalugg the wide lens item, meaning this move's accuracy goes up to 93.5%. And with this move set, I have an easy way to deal with Pokémon like King's Gambit, Garchomp, and Mega Charizard Y. Mawile is a great way to support Farigiraf, but sometimes against the right teams, I would much rather bring Mega Camerupt. When Camerupt mega evolves, it gets the ability sheer force, which is going to boost the power of all of our moves with a secondary effect by 30% while ignoring those effects at the same time. And for my move set, I'm running heat wave, earth power, and ancient power. Camerupt does get access to the move eruption, which starts at 150 base power, but this drops as Camerupt loses more health. And with sheer force, the base power of heat wave goes up to 123, so I feel like you're better off just using this move anyways. And then earth power goes up to 117 base power, which again makes it really good for dealing with Pokémon like Arcalidon or Toxapex. And then ancient power is just a good backup move to have for Pokémon like Charizard that can resist both of our hits.
