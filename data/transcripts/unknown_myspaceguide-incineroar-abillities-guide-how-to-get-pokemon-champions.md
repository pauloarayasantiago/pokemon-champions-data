# Incineroar Abillities Guide & How to Get - Pokemon Champions

**Channel:** MySpaceGuide | **Date:** unknown | **Views:** 240 | **Duration:** 1:37

---

Incineroar is absolutely insane in Pokemon champions right now, sitting at the number one spot in the monthly ranks with a massive 48% usage rate. This thing is the ultimate support pivot that fits into almost any team. If you want the best build, you have two main options. First is the standard support pivot. You're going to want a careful nature to boost that special defense. For your moves, run Fake Out, Flare Blitz, Parting Shot, and Snarl. This set is all about disruption. Your stat point spread should be 32 in HP, 16 in Defense, and 18 in Special Defense. Slap a Citrus Berry or Leftovers on it, and you're good to go. If you want more pressure, go with the offensive utility build. Use an adamant nature and run Fake Out, Flare Blitz, Knock Off, and Protect. For stats, go 14 HP, 20 Attack, 16 Defense, and 16 Special Defense. An Assault Vest works great here to keep you tanky while you dish out damage. Regardless of the build, you must run the Intimidate ability. It's broken for weakening physical attackers. For teammates, Incineroar is a god-tier partner for Dragonite and Garchomp because it helps them set up safely. It also works perfectly as a Sun supporter for Mega Charizard Y. Just watch out for counters like Prime Arena or Garchomp, which can bypass your Intimidate or hit you with heavy ground and water damage. Get your Incineroar ready and dominate the ladder.
