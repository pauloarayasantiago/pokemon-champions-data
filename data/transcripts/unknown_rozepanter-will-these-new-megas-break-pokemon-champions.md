Mega Heatran and Mega Tatsugiri are officially coming to Pokémon Champions in December and we finally know their abilities. Heatran gets a plus 2 defense boost by taking a hit it really doesn't want to and Tatsugiri actually gets an ability that might make me take this Mega actually serious. Beyond these two Pokémon we could see the return of some legendary Megas plus another Mega Pokémon that might be even more broken than all of them. Let's start with Mega Heatran. This Pokémon looks absolutely ridiculous. Now who decided that this Pokémon needed a Mega? It's already one of the greatest Pokémon of all time yet someone at Gamefreak was like yo we need to give Heatran this Mega it needs this Mega. I don't know who gaslighted the team into making this. 175 special attack and incredible defensive stats you don't even need to invest in special attack and you're still dealing insane damage you could just put everything in bulk. How is this Pokémon even real? Step Heat Wave for consistent spread damage, amazing matchup into a lot of meta-defining threats currently in Pokémon Champions, Mega Cherish and Y, Mega Floette, Mega Glitterbolt and those are just a few examples but does that mean it's broken because it does have some pretty serious weaknesses. First, Contruple weakness to ground big issue but also rain teams issue. Water attacks even doing even more damage but also weakens Heatran's fire type damage. So what ability did Mega Heatran actually get? Water compaction. When it gets hit by a water type move its defense goes up by two stages which is cool until you remember that it has to take a super effective water move which it doesn't want to take. I mean if you still have plenty of HP after physical attackers are not taking this Pokémon down aside from strong ground type attacks but if you take a big rain boosted water attack you will barely have any HP left and that defense boost is not going to help you against another water type attack either. I still think Mega Heatran can be a top threat. It already has the stats and very good matchups against a lot of Pokémon in the tier. I just don't think water compaction is a great ability on it and probably not a reason you'll be using it. And then we have Mega Tatsuguri. Dantozo is coming back too so this Pokémon might actually have something else to do aside from boosting Dantozo. Now if you look at the stats, garbage. For Mega Pokémon, garbage. It has good special attack and special defense but I expect that from a regular Pokémon these days. Speed is alright, you got step options, you got nasty plot, that's really it. So here's why I am actually investing in Mega Tatsuguri. Because it gets a new ability called Premium Great which raises its special attack by one stage whenever it enters the battle. And suddenly those horrible base stats actually look very threatening because I am not very excited about switching into a plus one Draco Meteor or Muddy Water. With the ability Commander you can still go for the classic Dantozo combination but once Dantozo goes down you get just Mega Eiffel of Tatsuguri and suddenly you now have to deal with another threat. Or you could just run Storm Drain on regular Tatsuguri, use it to punish water moves, especially rain themes on the switch. The rest of the stats, still garbage. Movepool, still pretty shallow. But Water Dragon Step is very difficult to switch into. Basically the only Pokémon here is Primarina that can do that. Beyond this I am very curious to see how this Pokémon ends up being in the metagame. I could see it not doing anything at all but also see it being very powerful. Alright next we might be getting two more legendary Mega Evolutions. Megaladios and Megaladios. Both base 700 stats total. So you expect these Pokémon to be insane but I am not really convinced. Megaladios, great offensive stats, solid bulk, pretty good movepool. No speed increase though. Is it really worth using your Mega slot on when there is already Mega Salamence which is faster, has better spread damage, can run a mixed set and regular ladios can still hit incredibly hard with items like life orb or soul dew. Now Megaladios though, that might actually be the more interesting of the two. Because instead of competing with the other offensive Megas it has incredible bulk and can play a more supportive role. Potentially making better use of moves like tailwind and helping hand. But even then you are still giving up your mega slot for a Pokémon that might not offer enough compared to the alternatives. So while both Megas are definitely usable, I honestly don't expect either of them to have a massive impact on Pokémon champions. Even 700 base total just doesn't cut it anymore. And finally, there is one more mega evolution that could potentially be the most broken of them all. Mega Magearna. Now this was supposedly leaked in a trailer.
