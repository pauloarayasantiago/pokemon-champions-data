Parish trap is a complicated but deadly team archetype. It literally does what it says in the name. Parish trap teams force the opposition into a lockdown via the use of Mega Gengar's shadow tag ability and the move Parish thong. This strategy puts the two rival Pokemon on a three- turn timer that forces them to faint when expired while pivoting defensive options like Incinor and Flip Turn Bask Legion in order to weaken the blows that Mega Gengar's teams receive. The idea is to force a numerical advantage and then win via inevitability against the last two Pokémon remaining on the opposing side. Such a devastating strategy has been able to win and get far in several tournaments, but it comes at a cost. For one, it is extremely mentally taxing for the Paris trap player as one mistake can often lead to defeat in much more catastrophic ways than typical straightforward strategies. Secondly, Paris trap is amongst the most hated BGC strategies by many of the players, which means that most teams prepare options in order to face them. Pokemon that are immune to fake out or intimidation can often put the pressure on Paris trap teams as an essential part of the strategy is debuffing the opposition. Paris trap teams also struggle with moves like taunt that can dismantle the strategy before it goes through. and even Pokemon like Komo O with its soundproof ability just naturally beat it. The strategy is quite similar to traditional Paris trap, but it tries to expedite the process by using self-destructing Pokemon like Lumbberry Snorlax, Ghost Curse or Destiny Bond users to take down the opposition and seal the deal with Parisong against the remaining two.
