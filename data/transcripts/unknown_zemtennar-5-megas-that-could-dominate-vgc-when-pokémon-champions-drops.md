# 5 Megas That Could Dominate VGC When Pokémon Champions Drops

**Channel:** ZemTennar | **Date:** unknown | **Views:** 2,884 | **Duration:** 13:36

---

With Pokemon Champions on the horizon, I thought it could be fun to talk about some Pokemon that could potentially dominate VGC when Champions drops. To do that, we are going to use some info from past generations, as well as some new info we've obtained from Pokemon Legends ZA about new mega forms and the way that these Pokemon may influence VGC's future. Before we get started, hit like and subscribe for more VGC content coming your way, and leave a comment if there is a Pokemon that you are excited for that I didn't mention in this video. Thank you, now let's begin. Dragonite has been a staple in Pokemon since the beginning. This pseudo-legendary was first introduced to the world in Japan all the way back in 1996, and with its new mega form, Dragonite might be entering its strongest era yet. Not only has it been around since the beginning, but it has also been a consistent VGC performer since then. We're talking 30-plus years of good Pokemon, and this Pokemon still holds up. In fact, it just received its mega form in 2025's Pokemon Legends ZA, meaning the Pokemon company still isn't done with it yet. To get a better look at what makes Dragonite so special though, let's take a look at the details and what it could bring about for the future of VGC. Dragonite currently has some of the most insane abilities in the game. They are so insane that the abilities have been copied multiple times, even on legendary Pokemon. Its first ability, Inner Focus, prevents flinching and keeps its stats from being lowered by the ability Intimidate. This already makes Dragonite amazing because Fake Out cannot flinch it, meaning it can just do whatever it wants the first turn without having to protect, and it can't have its attack lowered by Intimidate from a Pokemon like Incineroar. This makes Dragonite, especially back then, a no-brainer pick for your team, because being able to avoid those two things as a physical attacking Pokemon has endless value. Speaking of physical attacks, Dragonite's base attack stat is a whopping 134, giving it the ability to compete with some legendary Pokemon that are considered much stronger than it. Don't be surprised to see Dragonite facing off against a legendary or even beside them. Its second ability, Multiscale, is a much different ability. Multiscale currently reduces the damage Dragonite receives by 50%, so long as its HP is at full health. In my opinion, this ability isn't as strong as Inner Focus, but still has its place. If you were in a format where Intimidate and Fake Out weren't an issue, Multiscale would be amazing. We saw a ton of Multiscale usage on Dragonite in Pokemon Scarlet and Violet's Regulation H format, where this exact scenario happened because of hyper-offensive Pokemon, making Intimidators and Fake Out users pretty much irrelevant, which gave Dragonite's Multiscale value where it didn't have it before. Dragonite also receives a wide variety of moves, so its viability is pretty good on most teams, and it's also one of the only Pokemon to get extreme speed as a move, allowing you to bypass speed advantages and potentially take out the opponent before they even move so long as you aren't Ghost type, or have some way to avoid priority like with Indeedee's Psychic Surge. Like I said before, Dragonite just recently received its Mega Form in Pokemon Legends ZA, and with it, we can already see its upgraded identity forming. For starters, Dragonite's Mega Form has upgraded stats that at first glance can look a little bit confusing. It receives now 145 Special Attack as opposed to its base form, 100 Special Attack, and now its attack stat gets 124. It also received a boost in its speed stat by 20 points, making it 100 base speed, and its defensive stats were raised, with Defense now being 115, and Special Defense now being 125. This Pokemon becomes an even bigger powerhouse now in its Mega Form, and I wouldn't be surprised to see it appear on most teams in Pokemon Champions from the beginning based solely on this. The number one thing that I'm paying attention to, though, is that Dragonite now becomes a much more viable special attacker, and its Mega also allows its base form to now use Multiscale without having to worry about Intimidates because of this. I expect more trainers to lean into Dragonite's new Mega Form by building around its increased special attack. Keep in mind that we don't know yet what its Mega Form's abilities will be, so we will have to revisit this once we get more information, as well as the rest of the Pokemon on this list. I think that if Dragonite's Mega Form gets the ability Drizzle, this thing could be an amazing rain setter that makes its own Hurricane 100% accurate. This would also increase the power of all its water moves and make it even more resistant to fire types. This is just one of the things I can see that could make it shine, but like I said, we will have to see what the Pokemon Company does with this Mega. In my opinion, Dragonite is going to continue to be amazing and will probably dominate much of VGC coming up, and I can't wait to see what its new Mega can really do. Next on our list is Mega Skarmory, and the reason I wanted to feature this Pokemon here is that Skarmory is actually one of the Megas that interests me most, mainly because if it gets the ability I think that it might get, it would be a very dominant force in Pokemon champions. If you don't know anything about Skarmory, it was first introduced in Generation 2 of Pokemon and is a Steel Flying type in its original form. Steel Flying is already a pretty good typing, which we can see from other Pokemon that have done well in VGC like Corviknight, being a force to be reckoned with. Mega Skarmory seems to be taking this to another level. Its Bulbapedia entry states that Mega Skarmory's armor turns gold and its talons become super enlarged. It also states that Mega Skarmory can move faster than sound and finishes its opponents off with its sharp talons. The reason I am so interested in this Pokemon is because I think that Mega Skarmory could have the possibility of receiving two abilities with the first being Lightning Rod. The reason I believe this is because they changed Skarmory's armor to be gold. Gold in the regular world is known to be a conductor of electricity, which means electricity moves through it very easily. In the real world, gold is used in electronics most of us use every day. Phones, computers, circuit boards, chips. It is also used in space technology because it is resistant to heat and reflects radiation. Because of this, I believe that another ability it could get could be something similar to Baxcalibur's Thermal Exchange. This is mainly because Mega Skarmory's base stats give it 140 attack stat, and not being able to be burned would be a massive advantage for it to have. Mega Skarmory will be a force to be reckoned with no matter what happens, and if it gets either one of these abilities, it will make it even stronger. Mega Lucario was a huge surprise for me because I didn't originally think that they would give new Megas to Pokemon who already had Megas before. Lucario already had a Mega Form that made it faster at 112 base speed and hit harder with attack and 145 base attack. Now they have taken it a step further with Mega Lucario Z, and instead of increasing Lucario's attack, we now have a Lucario that will be good with special attacks. Mega Lucario Z's new special attack is base 164, and its new speed stat is 151. That's stronger and faster than its original Mega Form, and it doesn't have to worry about attack stat drops from Intimidate. This is a huge deal already, and we haven't even talked about the abilities they could potentially get yet. Before we get into that, one thing to understand is that most of Lucario's current viable special moves has the potential to lower the opponent's special defense, meaning it could potentially hit you in multiple ways that would make it stronger every single turn. This is a sort of pseudo way of getting a similar effect to Spectrier's ability Grimne, which increases its special attack every time it gets a knockout, except for Lucario, you don't need to knock the opponent out for this possibility to happen. Consider also that the rest of its viable moves either confuse the target or have the ability to flinch the opponent with Dark Pulse or Water Pulse. Now that we have that out of the way, we can deduce the possibility of two different but viable abilities it could get. The first one I believe it could be is Serene Grace. Serene Grace doubles the odds of a secondary effect of a move occurring, so Mega Lucario Z's 10% chance of lowering your special defense now becomes 20%, or Dark Pulse now has a 40% chance to flinch the opponent. This would make Mega Lucario Z a very annoying Pokemon to deal with and about as healthy for the game as Sleep Powder from Venusaur Under the Sun. If you don't like that idea, then I think another ability that would make it amazing and really fun to use would be Sheer Force. Sheer Force cancels the secondary effect of any move it learns and increases the power of those moves by times 1.3, essentially giving Mega Lucario Z a built-in life orb. This would make Mega Lucario Z a force to be reckoned with. We already saw how successful Sheer Force could be on Pokemon like Landorus, and having another fast special attacker like this would probably be no different. I actually hope for Sheer Force more here because I think a lot more people would use it if it does get this ability. Baxcalibur was introduced in Generation 9 of Pokemon, and we immediately saw how strong it was. It won the first regional in Scarlet and Violet, and it was just a powerhouse with 145 base attack. They've taken this a step further with Mega Baxcalibur. It is now bulkier than before with increased defense at 117 and increased special defense at 101. And the craziest stat increase is its attack stat going from 145 to 175. That's kind of insane. This Mega has been talked about already by a ton of YouTubers. A lot of people believe that Mega Baxcalibur could receive the ability Intrepid Sword, giving it an increased attack at plus one when it enters the field. This would make Mega Baxcalibur scary even for legendary Pokemon to face. The only plus side of going against this is that it's not very fast. Its base speed stat was kept at base 87, and there are plenty of Pokemon faster than that that could potentially mess it up before it has a chance to move. This is why I think its new ability will have something more to do with its defenses than it will increasing its attack. Considering they have already given it a massive attack boost, I believe that Mega Baxcalibur will receive or get something similar to the ability Ice Scales. Ice Scales have special damage, making it way more difficult for special attackers to take it down. Considering that its special defense stat is lower than its defense at 101, I believe this is much more possible than Intrepid Sword, as cool as that would be. Obviously, I could be wrong here, but to me, this just makes a lot more sense. This would make Mega Baxcalibur extremely difficult to take down with just one hit and give it the ability to dominate over all the new special attackers we are getting, but we will have to wait and see. Our final Pokemon on this list is Mega Absol Z. Absol was first introduced in generation three of Pokemon, and its most viable abilities have been Justified, which gives Absol plus one attack every time it is hit by a Dark-type move or Super Luck, which raises the critical hit ratio of the Pokemon's moves. Either way, Absol was increasing its ability to attack and its third ability, Pressure, leaned more into taking away the opponent's ability to attack by decreasing the PP of every move used against the Pokemon. Mega Absol Z seems to be taking this a step further. If we take a closer look at Mega Absol Z, you can see that its typing is different than its other forms. Mega Absol Z gets a crazy typing in Dark Ghost. The only other Pokemon that have received this typing in the past are Sableye and Spiritomb. This typing already does something special. It makes Mega Absol Z only weak to one type, Fairy-type Pokemon. This means that Mega Absol Z is much harder to take down than it seems right off the bat. I believe that the Pokemon company would lean more into this typing with its ability, whatever it ends up being, but it would be interesting if it got an ability that decreased Fairy damage or negated it completely, giving Mega Absol Z essentially no super effective weaknesses. Do I think they will actually do that? Probably not, but I wouldn't be too surprised if they did. By looking at its stats, Mega Absol Z is obviously a fast-frail attacker with high speed and attack stats, and I think that if any of these Megas will receive an attack-boosting ability, it would actually be Mega Absol Z. I believe there are three possibilities for abilities for Absol Z, and my picks would be Huge Power, Pure Power, or even better, Guts, meaning it gets Choice Band levels of an attack boost and ignores Burn Attack drops. This would make Mega Absol Z extremely hard to face considering how fast it is as well, at 151 base speed.
