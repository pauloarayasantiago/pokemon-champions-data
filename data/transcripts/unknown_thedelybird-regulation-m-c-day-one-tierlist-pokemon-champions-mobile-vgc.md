Welcome back to Zero to Hero. Today, we're talking about a day one tier list to regulation MC that just dropped in Pokémon Champions. Adding six new mega evolutions as well as a variety of other non-mega Pokémon and a few new items, it will change up the meta game a good bit. I'm only going to be talking about four tiers here and I'm only going to be mentioning Pokémon that I think are actually viable at all. So, anything not mentioned on this list, in my opinion, is pretty much unviable. We'll start off with the C tier and here we have, unfortunately, two of our new mega evolutions in Mega Absol and Mega Garchomp. Both of these megas have a base speed of 151, which is difficult to argue that they're bad. But, Absol, despite its good offenses, is so incredibly frail that it will fall to many neutral hits, not to mention a significant weakness to Incineroar not being able to hit it even neutrally with its stab attacks and being weak to intimidate. Unlike Mega Raichu, which had a similar set having incredible offense but being very frail, Mega Raichu has some other tools that Absol doesn't. First off, is better coverage in Focus Blast along with its electric moves, as well as the side effect of Zap Cannon paralyzing everything that it hits, and Raichu having access to fake out gives it quite a bit of utility along with the speed control from paralysis. Absol could use its high critical rate moves to crit through these intimidates, but Ghost and Dark isn't good offensive synergy in the first place and relying on crits to actually get your damage off is just sub-optimal. Garchomp, on the other hand, has a very weird stat spread. It's still relatively bulky thanks to its very high HP stat, despite its defenses going down to only 85 in both, but it also has a mixed offense with 130 attack and 141 special attack. This gives potential to open up a mixed set, perhaps running things like Draco Meteor and coverage moves like Flamethrower, while still using its physical ground and rock coverage, but losing its ground typing and losing stab on those ground attacks is just frankly bad for Garchomp. And it still faces a very bad match-up into the very prevalent Floette, not to mention a weakness to Incineroar's Intimidate, which will likely see more usage. Moving into the B tier, we have two more of our Mega Evolutions, Mega Lucario Z and Mega Baxcalibur. Mega Lucario Z's ability, Aura Guard, is amazing. Although its stats make it incredibly frail, I would clearly put this Mega above the other 151 speed Megas due to its better defensive typing and potential with its ability. Although its defensive stats are incredibly poor, with just some defensive investment, it can survive a lot of strong physical attacks that you wouldn't think it would. Things like Close Combat from Sneasler, Stomping Tantrum from Garchomp, and Flare Blitz from Incineroar, all can be survived with just a little bit of defensive investment. With its very high special offense and incredible speed, I could see it performing well. It still doesn't hit too hard due to its STAB moves in Flash Cannon and Aura Sphere being just 80 base power, but with access to something like Steel Beam, it could have immense immediate damage. However, this move does have anti-synergy with its low bulk. It also has other options like Nasty Plot and Calm Mind, and after getting just one of these off will allow it to hit incredibly hard with those 80 base power moves. Mega Baxcalibur on the other hand has a little bit of a mixed bag in terms of its matchups. While its stats across the board are incredible aside from its speed, meaning it will strongly benefit from some speed control, it has some interesting matchups into Floette and Charizard. It has a pretty good matchup into Charizard despite it being neutral to fire attacks. It maintains its Thermal Exchange ability, meaning it can get hit by a fire attack from Charizard, survive it thanks to its amazing stats, and easily respond with a one-hit KO attack boost. Even without any defensive investment, it will also survive a Dazzling Gleam from Floette, and it can EV to live a Moonblast from Floette, which is absolutely insane. And it could respond with a Iron Head and Ice Shard KO. Obviously, its matchup into Floette isn't a good one, but its stats are so incredible that I can still see it having some success. However, it's not as offensive as it seems. Its attack is amazing, but it still has tough matchups with things like Incineroar around and Kingambit still around resisting both of its stabs. It does about the same damage as regular Baxcalibur when it was holding a damage item. However, it does one-shot Charizard with Glaive Rush and it lives many fire attacks, which again can give it a boost from Thermal Exchange. And a Dragon Dance set could be very threatening from Baxcalibur, [music] not to mention some other combos like coaching from Sneasler, which will immediately boost its attack and defense, or even pairing it somehow with snow to boost its defense further. Pawmot is a non-Mega Pokémon that I think falls in the B tier. It has a pretty viable speed stat of 105, allowing it to outspeed the variety of 100-ish base speed Pokémon like Charizard, Floette, and Garchomp. It also has access to a very fast Fake Out, hits Charizard for super effective damage, and it can use Revival Blessing. Although using Revival Blessing is the new, this does work on Mega Evolved Pokémon and they will return to the match in their Mega Evolved state. Interesting to note, its signature move Double Shock kind of got an indirect buff with its ability as it is now considered a punching move and will be boosted by its Iron Fist ability. Moving into the A tier, we have two more of our Mega Evolutions. First up is Mega Gallade and Blaziken. Thankfully, this Pokémon did not stick with its old ability in Emergency Exit and got a new ability in Tough Claws. This will allow this Pokémon to hit incredibly hard and a Bug Steel is an amazing typing to have. Blaziken on its own and in a different metagame would definitely be an S tier pick. However, with its quad weakness to fire and with its Mega Charizard Y and Incineroar being so common, a lot of teams will have a way to easily one-shot Blaziken, meaning it will be very dependent on getting some good matchups and I could see it functioning far better on a dual Mega team rather than just on its own with the secondary Mega being able to cover those fire weaknesses. &gt;&gt; [music] &gt;&gt; It will work very good on rain teams as it can benefit from the rain to survive these four times super effective fire attacks and it will also be pretty good on Trick Room teams. Again, thanks for its low speed being just 40. Mega Salamence is my other A-tier pick. It's in a similar situation to Metagross when that was introduced in Regulation MB. Kingambit is already at the top of the format and will give Salamence [music] trouble. Not to mention Florges' Fairy presence is something that Mega Salamence didn't have to deal with in the past. Although Mega Salamence is threatened by Florges, it is faster than it and it can easily threaten a one-shot with a physical type Double-Edge boosted by Aerilate. And it does outspeed many of these other Mega Pokémon having a base speed of 120. I would say Salamence is borderline an S-tier pick as it still has a pretty good matchup into things like Charizard as well as most rain matchups, but with that Florges presence being so strong, as well as Kingambit being so prevalent as a Pokémon that will not only get boosted by Salamence's Intimidate, but it also resists both of Salamence's STABs. Again, I think this metagame just barely holds it back from S-tier. It can also run a purely physical set or a mixed set in the past with something like Hyper Voice that will get boosted by its Aerilate and deal some solid spread damage if its attack gets reduced. Not to mention options like Dragon Dance or Tailwind are both great options. Moving on to our S-tier, we have two picks in this tier. Our first and most obvious pick is Rillaboom. Obviously, it's Rillaboom. Rillaboom synergizes with a lot of Pokémon and it really enables balance archetypes along with Incineroar. Being a grass type, it is weak to Mega Charizard, which you think might hold it back from being an S-tier, but it does compete for terrain with Indeedee, which is our other S-tier pick. Setup Florges will be an amazing partner for Rillaboom as well as potential Perish Trap teams. And its matchup into opposing rain teams is also incredible, being able to hit Basculegions and Swamperts very hard while moving before them with Grassy Glide. We mentioned our other S-tier pick is Indeedee. Indeedee completely enables both hard Trick Room and Size Spam teams. Indeedee really is the ultimate Trick Room tool with type immunities, defensive tools in Follow Me, and newly added tools like Sing for some reason, as well as having access to Trick Room itself, it pairs very well with Hatterene for a solid Size spam trick room match up. Indeed email perhaps is A tier as it still pairs well with things like Sneasler or other Pokémon that can benefit from a psychic seed or a faster size spam mode maybe with something like Alakazam or Mega Gardevoir. So how will the previous meta game adapt to these changes? Well the first thought was Mega Raichu X could see some viability as it's able to compete for terrain. It could be very useful to deny opposing hard trick room teams that use Indeed as it can mega evolve after Indeed has already switched in removing the psychic terrain allowing Raichu itself to use fake out preventing trick room for that turn. It still seems like a sub optimal pick overall as it doesn't have any strong synergies other than the newly added electric seed. Rillaboom balance will by far be the biggest change in the meta game as it will significantly enable balance teams that like to set up with things like combine Floette. Perrish will also likely become more viable again with Indeed and Rillaboom both being able to use fake out and a pivot move along with the newly added eject button which is a staple on those teams. Rain will likely take a hit in usage again thanks to Rillaboom dealing so well with Basculin Swampert and Rillaboom in this format is likely to run an offensive item like miracle seed or a defensive item like grassy seed boosting its defense. Both of these will give it a much better match up into Basculin and Swampert than it would if it was running an assault vest and it's more likely to run these two items because assault vest still isn't in the game. Overall the mega evolution match up dynamic will be quite good as Salamence enters the fray which is pretty good into Mega Charizard Y but it also seems neutral or bad into Floette. Golisopod on the other hand has a very strong match up into Floette but a very bad match up into Charizard. So some of these new megas coming in do add a good mix up of match ups into these older megas. As mentioned with Indeed we will be seeing this Pokémon all over as trick room or size spam will absolutely be online and probably one of the main archetypes to watch out for. Compared to regulation MA where we had very few hard trick room tools now we have access to psychic terrain from Indeedee, who allow trick room to go up much easier, as well as life orb on Hatterene to allow it to hit very hard with size spam, and the classic charcoal Torkoal to hit hard with eruptions. Indeedee can even make use of the eject button itself to use follow me while Hatterene sets up trick room, immediately switching out and bringing in another trick room threat. Again, not to mention Mega Gallisopod could function very well on this team as well. Overall, the number one Pokémon that is the biggest winner from these changes, I believe, is Kingambit, which was already at the top of the meta game. It has a very good match-up into most of these new mega evolutions, having an incredible match-up into Mega Salamence, introducing more intimidate into the format for Kingambit to fight from, a good match-up into Baxcalibur, being able to one-shot with the steel moves, and a neutral match-up into Gallisopod, being able to go toe-to-toe with it while out speeding it. And it's also amazing into opposing size spam, or trick room, as its dark and steel typing allows it to completely wall those match-ups. That's all we have for today.
