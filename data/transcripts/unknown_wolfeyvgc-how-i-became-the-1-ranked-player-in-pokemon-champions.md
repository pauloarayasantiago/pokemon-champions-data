I tried to become the number one ranked player in Pokémon Champions, and I failed. I thought I'd have a decent chance at doing it using the Mega Meganium and Mega Kangaskhan team that I had built. And to be fair, it's not like the team itself was that bad, but it also wasn't that good. I tried modifying it a bunch, but it felt like no matter what I did, I was always stuck between winning and losing and just alternating pretty much every other game. I've done a lot of rank 1 challenges over the years, but I was kind of shocked by the difficulty of this one. In the past, I've even used themed teams to get rank 1, such as Red's team. So having no restrictions at all and still struggling felt like something I wasn't really prepared for. However, after thinking about it more, I realized that it actually made sense. This moment in Pokémon Champions is probably the most popular competitive Pokémon has ever been. And reaching the very top of the ladder, being ranked literally number 1 in the world is a very prestigious accomplishment. Over the last several days, many top players, including the defending world champion, as well as international champions and national champions from other regions, have held the spot at the very top. So it makes sense that a team that was good but not great wouldn't really be able to cut it. But even though it made sense, I was still feeling pretty demoralized. I feel like I've been in a bit of a slump when it comes to competitive Pokémon for several months now to be honest, pretty much since last August, and feeling like I was falling short yet again was pretty frustrating for me. However, I knew that I couldn't give up. So I decided to build a new team, around a new mega evolution. Mega Scovillain. Mega Scovillain is not thought to be a very good mega evolution. In fact, I would go so far as to say it's seen as something of a joke. It's a Pokémon with an incredible ability called Spicy Spray that burns any Pokémon that damages it, period. This is an incredible ability, but the problem is that Mega Scovillain gets a ton of stats in its offenses, and very few in its defenses. I think this is the reason why many players, myself included, didn't pay it much attention. However, right now in the current format, there's a core of Pokémon that are on the majority of teams. It's Sneezler, Basculegion, and King Gambit. And when I was thinking about Pokémon that could handle these three Pokémon, I was struck by how good Mega Scovillain is. Although it is weak to Sneezler, it matches up pretty well into King Gambit, and it also is neutral to Basculegion's water-type moves. More importantly, all three of these Pokémon are physical attackers, meaning they don't like to be burned because it will cut their attack stat in half. I was also struck by Scovillain's Fire typing, giving it a resistance to Fairy-type attacks. However, Mega Scovillain is also a Grass-type, and can use the move Leech Seed. Leech Seed steals 12% of a target's health every single turn, and although this damage doesn't sound like that much on its own, I realized that with Burn, it can stack up really quickly.
