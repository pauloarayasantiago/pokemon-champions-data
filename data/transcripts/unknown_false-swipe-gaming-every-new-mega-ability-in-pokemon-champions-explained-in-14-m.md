Upon Mega Evolving, Clefable trades in its base forms ultra resilient Magic Guard for another magic ability. Magic Bounce similarly makes Mega Confable impossible to wear down with status moves. But rather than making it immune to the effects of moves like toxic and leech seed, it now actually bounces them back at the opponent using them. That's not all though, as it is now similarly unaffected by and bounces back moves dealing non-damaging status conditions like sleep powder or thunderwave and all status moves in general. You can't taunt or encore it anymore. You'll only wind up using those moves against yourself. The downside is that without magic guard, Mega Cliffable can actually be affected by passive damage like sandstorm and burns from damaging fire moves. Next up, we have Mega Victory Bell and Inners Out. When a Pokemon with inners out is KO'ed by an attacking move, the user of that move takes damage equivalent to that inflicted on the target for the KO. For example, if Pukumuku, the only Pokemon to have Innerdaught previously, was at 150 HP when it was KOed by a Ldios using Draco Meteor, Ldios would also take 150 HP of damage. This is definitely a cool ability with lots of potential, but not one really suited to a mega, as you don't generally want the Pokemon you've dedicated your mega slot to to have to faint before its ability does anything.
