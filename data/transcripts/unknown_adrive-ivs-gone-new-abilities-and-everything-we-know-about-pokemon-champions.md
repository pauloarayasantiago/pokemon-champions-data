Let's address the biggest bombshell first. Individual values, aka IVs, are no longer in Pokemon Champions. Every Pokemon functions as if it has a 31 IV in every single stat. There is no way to lower them, no way to adjust them. They simply don't exist as a variable anymore. For casual and newcomers, this is a massive win. No more breeding for perfect IVs. No more hyper training, no more checking IVs at the judge function. Every Pokemon you obtain is a stat ready from the moment you get it. But for competitive veterans, this is where things get nuanced. The removal of IVs eliminates a layer of strategic depth that a lot of high level players relied on. Here are some of the specific interactions that are gone. First, trick room speed control. In the old system, players would deliberately run zero speed IVs on a slow Pokemon to guarantee they move first under trick room. With every Pokemon now at max speed IVs, speed ties in trick room become much more common and much less controllable. You can't under speed a mirror anymore. Second, foul play defense. Foul play calculates damage using the target's attack stat. Special attackers would deliberately run zero attack IVs to reduce incoming foul play damage, but that's no longer possible. And third, ability boost manipulation. Pokemon with beast boost, proto synthesis, and cork drive boost their highest stat. Competitive players would sometimes lower specific IVs to control which stat got boosted. For example, getting speed focus beast boost on kartana required lower than max attack IVs. That kind of fine tuning is gone. Fourth, gyro ball power. Gyro ball's damage is based on how much slower the user is in the target. With all slow Pokemon now at max speed IVs, gyro ball does less damage across the board. Some matchups that previously were two AKOs become three AKOs. Serebii's preview put it well. Whether this are good or bad depends entirely on how much you min max your stats for these niche but important interactions.
