# Singles Meta Snapshot - Pokemon Champions 3v3 Ladder

_Current as of 2026-04. Champions supports both Doubles (VGC, 4v4 pick from 6) and Singles (3v3 pick from 6). This project is Doubles-focused; this doc covers Singles only for when users ask directly. Pikalytics usage_ numbers below are not in our CSV (which is Doubles-only); figures are sourced from in-game battle spot data as quoted by content creators and should be treated as qualitative unless refreshed._

## Format Basics

- **Singles 3v3 pick from 6.** Same 186 fully-evolved roster + Pikachu as Doubles; same 59 Mega Evolutions; same item pool (no Life Orb, Choice Band/Specs, Rocky Helmet, Eviolite, Heavy-Duty Boots, etc.).
- **Stealth Rock and hazard stack are viable** (unlike Doubles where hazards are less impactful). Hippowdon, Archaludon, Garchomp, Gamora are common setters.
- **Screens at full 50% reduction** (not nerfed to 33% like Doubles). Mega Froslass Aurora Veil is correspondingly stronger.
- **Spread moves do full damage** (no 0.75× multiplier that applies in Doubles). Earthquake, Surf, Heat Wave hit one target at full power.
- **Fake Out still usable turn 1**, but less central — fewer pivot opportunities, and slow Pokemon aren't sheltered by a second ally.
- **Trick Room less prominent** — setter has to survive a 1v1 turn to set; no Farigiraf Armor-Tail redirection layer.
- **Protect is 8 PP** (same nerf as Doubles). Stalling is harder than in prior formats; Toxapex-style PP stall is neutered.
- **Incineroar lost Knock Off and U-turn** (same as Doubles). Pivot role falls to Corviknight, Rotom-Wash, Hydreigon, Meowscarada.

## Common Sets (Creator-Sourced)

### Garchomp (Sweeper Lead)
- **Ability:** Rough Skin
- **Item:** Focus Sash (or Lum Berry)
- **Nature:** Jolly
- **Moves:** Earthquake / Outrage / Scale Shot / Poison Jab
- **Note:** Scale Shot is viable even without Loaded Dice (banned in Champions). Poison Jab preferred over Iron Head vs Primarina (1×) and Meganium (4×).

### Primarina (Offensive Wallbreaker)
- **Ability:** Torrent (preferred over Liquid Voice for Sparkling Aria crit chance and flat higher power)
- **Item:** Focus Sash or Mystic Water
- **Nature:** Modest (offensive)
- **Moves:** Moon Blast / Sparkling Aria / Aqua Jet / Encore
- **Alt:** Calm Mind / Moon Blast / Sparkling Aria / Aqua Jet (slower setup variant)

### Corviknight (U-turn Pivot)
- **Ability:** Mirror Armor / Pressure
- **Item:** Leftovers
- **Moves:** Roost / U-turn / Iron Head (or Brave Bird) / Body Press (or a 4th coverage)
- **Note:** Iron Defense + Body Press + U-turn + Roost is a popular set but weaker than U-turn + Iron Head per `temp6t` because Ghost types wall it and slow U-turns need immediate offensive threat.

### Hippowdon (Stealth Rock Wall)
- **Ability:** Sand Stream
- **Item:** Citrus Berry or Leftovers
- **Nature:** Impish
- **Moves:** Stealth Rock / Earthquake / Yawn (or Whirlwind) / Slack Off
- **Note:** Whirlwind preferred over Roar because Whirlwind ignores Soundproof.

### Archaludon (Special Setter/Attacker)
- **Ability:** Stamina (preferred) or Sturdy
- **Item:** Leftovers or Citrus Berry
- **Nature:** Modest
- **Moves:** Stealth Rock / Draco Meteor / Flash Cannon / Thunderbolt (or Electro Shot if rain-pivot)
- **Note:** Archaludon lost Body Press in Champions. Sphere (fighting move, 90 BP) is the weak substitute.

### Kingambit (Physical Sweeper)
- **Ability:** Supreme Overlord (preferred over Defiant for sweeps; Defiant on specific anti-Intimidate teams)
- **Item:** Black Glasses (dominant; no Life Orb available)
- **Nature:** Adamant
- **Moves:** Sucker Punch / Kowtow Cleave / Iron Head / Swords Dance
- **Alt:** Replace Swords Dance with Stealth Rock on a non-sweep role — sub-1% usage but viable as Steel-type hazard setter that beats Gamora's Mortal Spin.

### Mimikyu (Disguise Sweeper)
- **Ability:** Disguise
- **Item:** Spell Tag (preferred — no Life Orb, Spell Tag powers Shadow Claw + Shadow Sneak with no HP cost)
- **Nature:** Adamant or Jolly
- **Moves:** Play Rough / Shadow Claw / Swords Dance / Shadow Sneak
- **Alt:** Scope Lens + Shadow Claw for crit fishing (viable per HoshinJosh); Destiny Bond or Curse as 4th on bulky sets.
- **Note:** Do not run Poltergeist — move fails if target has no item, and items like Lum/Sash get consumed mid-game.

### Aegislash (Stance Change)
- **Ability:** Stance Change
- **Item:** Spell Tag or Leftovers
- **Moves:** Shadow Sneak / Sacred Sword (or Close Combat) / Swords Dance / King's Shield
- **Mixed alt:** Shadow Ball / Flash Cannon / Close Combat / King's Shield
- **Note:** Poltergeist popular but item-dependent (same caveat as Mimikyu). Sacred Sword preferred over Close Combat for no-drawback consistency.

### Hydreigon (Scarf Pivot)
- **Ability:** Levitate
- **Item:** Choice Scarf
- **Nature:** Timid
- **Moves:** Draco Meteor / Dark Pulse / U-turn / Fire Blast (or Flamethrower, or Earth Power)
- **Weakness:** Primarina walls all 4 moves (immune to Dark via Fairy, resists Dragon, resists Fire). Pair with Gengar or a fighting type to break it.

### Meowscarada (Scarf Pivot)
- **Ability:** Protein (explicit — Overgrow is "bad")
- **Item:** Choice Scarf
- **Moves:** Flower Trick / Triple Axel / Knock Off / U-turn
- **Note:** Scarf Meowscarada outspeeds Adamant Excadrill in Sand Rush.

## Key Differences From Doubles

| Axis | Doubles (VGC) | Singles (3v3) |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Pick | Bring 6, play 4 | Bring 6, play 3 |
| Spread moves | 0.75× damage | Full damage (no multiplier) |
| Screens | 33% reduction | 50% reduction |
| Aurora Veil strength | Moderate | Very strong (no halving) |
| Fake Out | Game-defining turn-1 tool | Usable but less central |
| Intimidate | Spammed on lead pairs | Single switch-in, swaps reset |
| Trick Room | Hard archetype (~64% WR) | Niche — setter must 1v1 survive |
| Weather wars | Every archetype | Less core; weather picked per matchup |
| Stealth Rock | Rarely viable | Core setup for most teams |
| Redirection (Rage Powder / Follow Me) | Sinistcha central | Irrelevant — no partner |
| Priority | Fake Out + Extreme Speed | Sucker Punch + Aqua Jet + Shadow Sneak more impactful |
| Wallbreakers vs Walls | Balanced | Wallbreaker-favored |

## Archetype Sketch (Non-Exhaustive)

- **Hyper Offense:** Garchomp lead → Kingambit + Mimikyu sweepers. Sash to guarantee setup turn. Stealth Rock backup from Archaludon or Hippowdon.
- **Sand Balance:** Hippowdon (Stealth Rock + Sand Stream) + Garchomp (Sand Veil or Rough Skin) + Tyranitar (Mega) or Excadrill. Rock + Ground + Dark coverage.
- **Pivot Core:** Corviknight (U-turn) + Hydreigon (Scarf U-turn) + Meowscarada (Scarf U-turn). Chain slow pivots into an offensive Mega (Gengar, Dragonite, Charizard Y).
- **Fairy Sweep:** Mega Floette (Calm Mind, Fairy Aura) + Espathra (Speed Boost Baton Pass) + a priority/redirect shell (Rotom-Wash for Willow-Wisp + Volt Switch, Hisuian Arcanine for Intimidate + Flare Blitz). Source: HoshinJosh ranking team. Team code `H7BAYK88GB` (creator's shared ID — verify in-game).
- **Aurora Veil:** Mega Froslass (Snow Warning + Aurora Veil) + setup sweeper (Kingambit, Mimikyu, Palafin). Snow screens give full 50% mitigation.
- **Scarf Bulky Offense:** Scarf Hydreigon + Scarf Dragapult + bulky lead (Slowking, Corviknight, Milotic).

## Common Threats

1. **Primarina** — top-2 usage; Moon Blast + Sparkling Aria is wide coverage; walls Hydreigon. Answer: Amoonguss (wait — not in roster), Venusaur, Corviknight, Kingambit, steel coverage.
2. **Garchomp** — top-1 usage; Earthquake + Outrage is wide coverage. Answer: Primarina, Corviknight, Gliscor, Dragonite (Multiscale), Fairy types.
3. **Mega Floette (Eternal)** — massive Fairy Aura Moon Blast. Answer: Kingambit, Aegislash, Mega Scizor, Sneasler Dire Claw, poison types.
4. **Scarf Hydreigon** — U-turn pivot with fire/dragon/dark nukes. Answer: Primarina (walls all 4 common moves), Azumarill, Sylveon.
5. **Scarf Dragapult** — Dragon Darts ignores Focus Sash on single-target. Answer: Kingambit (sturdy-ish to Dragon Darts + STAB Sucker Punch kills), Tyranitar (Sand Stream chip).
6. **Mega Scizor** — Technician Bullet Punch priority. Answer: Rotom-Wash (Will-O-Wisp), Hisuian Arcanine (Flare Blitz), Mega Charizard Y.
7. **Setup sweepers** (Mimikyu Swords Dance, Kingambit Swords Dance, Gyarados Dragon Dance, Feraligatr Mega) — answered by Unaware walls, phasing (Whirlwind/Roar), priority revenge (Sucker Punch, Aqua Jet).

## Qualitative Notes

- Singles is reportedly **harder to rank up in than Doubles** per ladder players (e.g. HoshinJosh, commenting on social media buzz): more players on the singles ladder because singles is the "default" Pokemon mode new players gravitate toward, plus no team-preview meta-game to exploit.
- **No legendaries / no restricteds / no paradox** — same roster as Doubles. Singles meta is constrained to "fully-evolved 186 + Pikachu" and is smaller than recent BSS formats.
- **Pikachu has no Light Ball** in the current item pool, so Pikachu is essentially unused (same as Doubles).
- **Mega Evolution limit is still one per team** — standard Mega pick applies (Charizard Y/X, Dragonite, Gengar, Scizor, Froslass, Floette, Mimikyu, Feraligatr, Tyranitar, Meganium are common Mega choices in singles).
